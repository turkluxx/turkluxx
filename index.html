CITIZENSHIP BY INVESTMENT

Turkish Citizenship Through Real Estate Investment

Purchasing qualifying real estate in Türkiye may provide a pathway to Turkish citizenship through the exceptional citizenship programme.

USD 400,000+
Minimum qualifying real-estate investment.
3 YEARS
The qualifying property must carry a restriction preventing its sale for at least three years.
FAMILY
The exceptional citizenship framework can also cover the applicant's spouse and eligible children, subject to the applicable requirements.
OFFICIAL PROCEDURE
Eligibility and citizenship are determined by the relevant Turkish authorities. Purchasing a property does not by itself constitute an automatic grant of citizenship.
