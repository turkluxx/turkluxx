01 About TurkLuxx

TurkLuxx is a property marketing and buyer-introduction company.

TurkLuxx markets selected property opportunities and facilitates introductions and communications between prospective buyers and property developers and/or their authorized representatives.

TurkLuxx is not the property developer or seller unless expressly identified as such in a separate written agreement.

TurkLuxx does not itself conduct or complete the property purchase transaction.

Unless expressly stated in a separate written agreement, TurkLuxx does not:

  • Receive or hold property purchase funds
  • Receive or hold deposits
  • Operate escrow accounts
  • Process purchase payments
  • Conduct property closings
  • Transfer title
  • Execute purchase contracts on behalf of the buyer or developer

  • Approve mortgages or loans

  • Provide tax services
  • Provide immigration services
