01 Introduction

Welcome to TurkLuxx.

TurkLuxx is a property marketing and buyer-introduction company. We market selected property opportunities and connect prospective buyers with property developers and/or their authorized representatives.

This Privacy Policy & Privacy Notice explains how TurkLuxx collects, uses, stores, protects, and may disclose personal information when you visit our website, submit an inquiry, request property information, communicate with us, or otherwise interact with our marketing and introduction services.

This Privacy Policy is intended to provide transparency regarding our information practices. Additional rights or requirements may apply depending on your location and applicable law.

02 Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect information such as:

Information you provide directly

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • WhatsApp or other messaging contact information
  • Country or region of residence
  • Property preferences
  • Preferred location
  • Approximate budget or price range
  • Preferred property type
  • Information contained in your inquiries or communications
  • Appointment or viewing requests
  • Other information you voluntarily provide

Information collected automatically

When you visit our website, we may collect certain technical information, including:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited
  • Date and time of visits
  • Referring website
  • General website interaction information
  • Approximate location derived from technical information

We may collect certain information through cookies, analytics technologies, advertising technologies, and similar tools.

Registered referral links contain partner and participant identifiers. These identifiers are remembered for the browser tab session and included with an enquiry you submit to associate it with the referring participant. A referral visit does not identify a buyer or prove a sale.

03 How We Use Personal Information

We may use your information to:

  • Respond to your property inquiry
  • Provide information about properties marketed through TurkLuxx
  • Connect you with a relevant developer or authorized representative
  • Facilitate communications between you and the relevant property provider
  • Arrange introductions, consultations, or property-related meetings
  • Respond to questions and requests
  • Improve our website and services
  • Analyze website usage
  • Measure marketing and advertising performance
  • Send relevant marketing communications where permitted
  • Maintain website security
  • Detect and prevent fraud or abuse
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

TurkLuxx does not use your personal information to make legal, tax, immigration, financial, or investment determinations on your behalf.

04 Sharing Information With Developers and Authorized Representatives

A central purpose of TurkLuxx's service is connecting prospective buyers with property developers and/or their authorized representatives.

If you request information about a particular property or otherwise ask us to facilitate an introduction, we may provide relevant information to the applicable developer or authorized representative so that they can respond to your inquiry.

The developer or other recipient may independently process your personal information under its own privacy practices and policies.

We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any developer or other third party to whom you are introduced.

Communications may be sent by email, telephone, SMS/text message, WhatsApp, or other contact methods you provide. For SMS/text messages, message frequency varies and message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of text messages or HELP for assistance. You may opt out of marketing communications at any time. Opting out of marketing communications does not prevent TurkLuxx from responding to an existing inquiry, appointment, request, or other service-related matter through a legally permitted method of communication.
